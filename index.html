ABOUT US

Tristan, Isaac, Matthew, Eric, and Shayaan — these 5 entities have united to make sure just about anyone can keep up with the T.I.M.E.S.. Recently, the adoption of mindsets that inhibit growth among students has become exceedingly hard to ignore. Time after time again, innocent, terrifyingly approachable untruths seed within the fragile little minds of up and coming university students.

By analyzing these great untruths as presented in The Coddling of the American Mind alongside the major untruths illustrated in Plato's Allegory of the Cave, we five sincerely hope to bring enlightenment to our dear viewers.

Letting oneself experience discomfort, doing away with in-groups and out-groups, and treating emotions as secondary — indeed, that is how we at T.I.M.E.S can help our viewers keep up with the times.
